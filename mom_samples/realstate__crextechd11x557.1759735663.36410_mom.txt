Main Topic/Objective
Investment opportunity in a commercial project in Gurugram

Key Points Discussed
The project is a mall being built in Gurugram near the Delhi-Gurugram border
The investment amount is 50 lakhs for a 150 square foot shop
The investor will receive 12% assured return on their investment for 3 years
After 3 years, the investor will receive rental income from the shop
The investor needs to pay 60% of the investment amount upfront and the remaining 40% after 2-3 years
The project is expected to be completed in 3 years

Decisions Made or Action Items
Pooja will send the details of the project to the investor
The investor will review the details and decide if they want to invest in the project
The investor will contact Pooja if they have any questions or want to proceed with the investment
