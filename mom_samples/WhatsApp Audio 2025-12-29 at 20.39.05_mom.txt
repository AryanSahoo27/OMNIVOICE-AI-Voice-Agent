Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of discussion is the Western Residential project by Titan Infrastructures, specifically the details and pricing of the 3 BHK and 4 BHK units in the project.

Key Points Discussed
The project is located in Sector 103, on the Dwarka Express Road, and consists of 21 acres of land with 13 high-rise towers. The units are available in sizes ranging from 2600 to 3400 square feet, with prices starting at ₹25,000 per square foot for 3 BHK and ₹26,000 per square foot for 4 BHK. The discussion also touches on the concept of branded residences and how they are more appreciated in terms of value. The current market price in the area is around ₹15,000 to ₹17,000 per square foot, but the project's price is higher due to its luxury features and brand value. The discussion also involves the discounts available, including an initial discount of 11% and a time-bound discount of ₹750, as well as a channel partner discount of up to 4%.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The decision made during the discussion is to consider the project's pricing, taking into account the available discounts, and to potentially close the deal at a price of around ₹21,800 per square foot, with the total price for a 3 BHK unit, including GST, being around ₹5.80 lakhs. The action item is to further discuss the pricing and come to a mutually agreeable decision.
