Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of discussion is advertising options for a new company, specifically on autos in Jaipur, and the objective is to explore different advertising methods and their prices.

Key Points Discussed
The key points discussed include three types of auto advertising: 
1. Auto pay with vinyl material, 1.5 by 3 feet in size, costing ₹100 per auto for one month, with a minimum of 200 autos.
2. Multi-permanent tattoo, 10 by 24 inches in size, made of reflective material, costing ₹150 per auto for three months.
3. Auto hood cover, made of rexine material, costing ₹550 per auto for three months, with a minimum of 200 autos.
Additionally, cab branding was also discussed, with a cost of ₹2000 per cab for one month.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The decisions made or action items include the consultant sending samples and prices of the three advertising options to the client via WhatsApp, and the client will review the samples and contact the consultant if they have any doubts or questions.
