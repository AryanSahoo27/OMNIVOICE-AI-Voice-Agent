Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of the conversation is to inquire about the status of a refund request made by Manoj Kumar Sharma on 17th. 

Key Points Discussed
The customer, Manoj Kumar Sharma, had made a refund request on 17th and has not received the refund yet. 
The customer service representative asked for the customer's name and salesperson's name to check the status of the refund request. 
The customer provided the required information, and the representative asked the customer to wait for 10 minutes to check the status. 
The customer expressed frustration about the delay in processing the refund and asked to speak with a manager. 
The representative explained that the refund process may take 10-15 days, but the customer was not satisfied with the explanation.

Decisions Made or Action Items

The customer asked to speak with a manager to get a clearer explanation of the refund process and the expected timeline for receiving the refund.
