Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of the conversation is a complaint about a washing machine that was purchased from DG, and the customer is discussing the issue with a representative from the company.

Key Points Discussed
The customer had previously complained about the issue and was given a contact number to call for assistance. The representative informed the customer that a technician would visit their location to check the washing machine. The customer was told that there would be a service charge of 1000, and if any parts needed to be replaced, the cost would be additional. The representative explained that the technician would check the machine, determine the cost of any necessary parts, and then inform the customer of the total cost.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The technician will visit the customer's location the next day to check the washing machine. The customer will be informed of the total cost of any necessary repairs, including the cost of any replacement parts. The customer will need to pay the service charge of 1000, as well as the cost of any replacement parts. The representative assured the customer that the same technician who visits to check the machine will also be the one to replace any parts, and that there will be no additional charge for labor.
