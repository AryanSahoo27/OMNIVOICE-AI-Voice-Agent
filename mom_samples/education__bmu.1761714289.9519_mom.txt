Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of discussion is the admission process for PhD at a university, specifically the requirements, fees, and entrance exam.

Key Points Discussed
The key points discussed include the online application process, registration fees, entrance exam, and research paper submission. The discussion also covered the attendance requirements, with the applicant being informed that daily classes are not mandatory, and the exam schedule, which includes one exam per year.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The decisions made or action items include the applicant applying online, paying the registration fees, and preparing for the entrance exam. The applicant was also advised to visit the university for further clarification on the admission process and to confirm the details discussed during the conversation. The university representative informed the applicant that the entrance exam is the primary criteria for admission, and the applicant's chances of getting admitted will depend on their performance in the exam.
