Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of discussion is the Western residential project, specifically the prices and features of the apartments.

Key Points Discussed
The key points discussed include the box price of the apartments, which starts at 6.5 CR for a 3 BHK and 26,000 for a 4 BHK. The project is spread over 21 acres and has 13 high-rise towers. The apartment sizes range from 2673 to 4300 square feet. The price of a 4 BHK apartment is approximately 10.5 CR. The project has internal and external facing apartments, with the internal facing apartments having a higher price due to the premium location. The PLC (preferential location charge) is applicable for internal facing apartments and ranges from 4-6% of the box price.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The decision made is to schedule a meeting to discuss the project and prices in more detail. The consultant will send a WhatsApp message with more information about the project, including a brochure and price details. The client is expected to visit the project site in Gurgaon to get a better understanding of the project.
