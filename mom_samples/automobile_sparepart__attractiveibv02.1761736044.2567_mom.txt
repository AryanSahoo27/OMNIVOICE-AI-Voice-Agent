Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of discussion is the requirement of a new battery and tire for a Bajaj Pulsar 125 vehicle.

Key Points Discussed
The customer is looking for a battery and tire for their Bajaj Pulsar 125, the battery size and type are discussed, the customer is informed that the battery price will depend on the size, the tire size is 2117 and the price is 1550 rupees, the customer is also informed about the warranty and manufacturing details of the tire.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The customer will visit the store to check the battery size and the store will provide the battery and tire as per the customer's requirement, the store will also provide a 3-year unconditional warranty and 5-year manufacturing warranty for the tire, the customer will get the tire fitted at the store and the fitting charges will be included in the price.
