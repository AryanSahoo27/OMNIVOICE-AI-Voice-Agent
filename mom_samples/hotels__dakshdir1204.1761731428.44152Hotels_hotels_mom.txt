Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of the conversation is to inquire about the availability and pricing of a resort for a family of 15 members for a one-night stay on the first of November.

Key Points Discussed
The key points discussed in the conversation include the availability of the resort, the pricing per person, the inclusion of meals, and the activities available at the resort. The resort has two options: Dax Resort and Amusement Park, and Dax Eden Greens. The pricing is 2200 per person, which includes lunch, dinner, and breakfast. Some activities are chargeable, while others are complimentary. The resort also offers a complimentary dance event if the occupancy is 60-70%.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The decision made in the conversation is that the customer will send a message to the resort to confirm the booking, and the resort will share the details and offer the best possible discount. The customer is interested in booking the resort, but the final decision is pending the confirmation of the details and the discount offered by the resort.
