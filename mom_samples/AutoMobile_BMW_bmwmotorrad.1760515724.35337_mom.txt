Main Topic/Objective
The main topic of the conversation is to discuss the servicing of a 2021 BMW model car and to get an approximate quotation for the routine service.

Key Points Discussed
The car owner is planning to purchase a 2021 BMW model and wants to know the estimated cost of routine servicing, which is expected to be around 10,000 to 12,000. The service advisor informs that they can provide a quotation without the car being physically present at the service center. The owner shares the car's number and the service advisor agrees to send an approximate quotation via WhatsApp. The owner also inquires about the routine check-up and maintenance of the car, including the replacement of air filters, oil filters, and engine oil.

Decisions Made or Action Items
The service advisor will send an approximate quotation for the routine service of the car via WhatsApp. The owner will provide the car's details, including the number, once the car is transferred to their name. The service advisor will check the car's history and provide a detailed report on the maintenance and replacement of parts, including air filters, oil filters, and engine oil.
